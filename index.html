How much does a collaboration cost?

A story is PKR 1,000, a promotional reel is PKR 6,000 and an interview reel is PKR 10,000. For anything else, send me the deliverables and I'll quote a custom package.

How long does a reel take?

Timelines are agreed per project once the concept is locked. Tell me your deadline up front and I'll confirm whether it works.

Can we use the content in our own ads?

Yes. Ad rights are sold separately from the content itself and priced by how many months you want them for.

Do you work with brands outside Karachi?

Yes. Products can be shipped for content, and I travel for events and activations across Pakistan.
